    How switching works

    A switch changes the login for the whole VM. Running T3 threads move to the new Claude account on their next request; Codex needs a new thread. Auto-switch moves to the next account at the threshold and back once the preferred one's limit resets. To add an account, log in with it (/login in Claude Code, codex login); it shows up here within 2 minutes.
